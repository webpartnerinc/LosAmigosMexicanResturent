Since 1998

A Small Town Table With Big Flavor

Los Amigos opened its doors on Ephraim's 700 South in 1998 with a simple idea: serve the kind of Mexican food our family loves to cook, in portions that leave nobody hungry, at prices a small town can afford.

Nearly thirty years later, the kitchen still runs the same way. Recipes come from family tradition, our salsas are made in-house, and every order is cooked fresh. Regulars greet regulars across the dining room, and first-timers usually don't stay first-timers for long.

In 2026, the people of Sanpete County voted Los Amigos 1st Place for Best Mexican Food and 1st Place for Best Taco — an award that belongs as much to our guests as to our kitchen. ¡Gracias, amigos!

Whether you're a Snow College student hunting a study-break burrito, a family claiming the big table for a birthday, or just passing through the valley, there's a seat waiting for you at Los Amigos.
